# Research Quality Override

Effective 2026-08-18, scientific strength and defensibility outrank convenience.

The target is a rigorous master's thesis whose core experiments can be completed through intensive work and automation in approximately 4–6 weeks. The schedule does not authorize weak baselines, omitted ablations, superficial novelty claims, or a simple XMEAS-versus-XMEAS+XMV feature comparison.

No topic may be locked until the scientific gate covers recent prior work, objective variables and ground truth, falsifiable hypotheses, baselines, ablations, robustness, statistics, failure modes, workload, and explicit GO/MODIFY/KILL criteria.

Candidate A may survive only as a mechanism-level study. Candidate B may survive only if post-retrieval applicability verification is formally and experimentally distinguishable from similarity-, covariate-, regime-, and control-aware retrieval. A combined direction is admissible only as one coherent research question, with Candidate A serving as evidence or ablation rather than a second thesis topic.
